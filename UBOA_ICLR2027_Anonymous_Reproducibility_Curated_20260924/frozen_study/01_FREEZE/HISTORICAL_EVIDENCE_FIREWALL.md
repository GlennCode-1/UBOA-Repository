The following evidence may motivate the design but cannot appear in the fresh confirmatory sample:
- all 432,000 Route-A synthetic records and all derived policy comparisons;
- the external process challenge;
- the six UCI prospective-cohort outcomes;
- Weather/ETTm2 and other earlier OOS results;
- Route-1 deterministic theorem checks and the analytic informative/vacuous examples;
- any output from Route-2 research.

No old random seed, saved simulated path, prediction cache, fitted model, OOS loss, or summary numerator/denominator may be copied into `09_OUTPUTS`.

The study may cite theorem formulas and deterministic parameter calculations from development, because they are mathematical specification rather than fresh empirical evidence.
